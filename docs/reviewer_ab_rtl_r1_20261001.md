# Reviewer A/B selected-winner RTL validation (October 1, 2026)

The September 25 reviewer A/B search has 324 saved arms and 161/162 audit-valid matched pairs over 27 HLSFactory kernels, but its selected winners had no executed RTL co-simulation. This separate campaign validates a predeclared first-repeat cohort: both review arms for every kernel under both flash and dynamic modes. It includes gains, regressions, ties, and parent-retained selections without selecting by the reported effect. The cohort contains **54 matched pairs / 108 arms / 102 distinct source-and-harness RTL jobs**; six repeated source/input combinations share a measurement. The other two repeats remain unvalidated and the full 324-arm estimate remains a C-synthesis estimate until separately checked.

All 108 arms had passed the original reference-isolation audit, feasibility gate, native independent golden check, and candidate C simulation. Preparation verified the saved source SHA-256, its matching synthesis event or frozen-parent report, the original result and input fingerprints, and the benchmark's versioned shape registry. The frozen September 28 historical validator then reruns native independent golden, candidate C simulation, and Vitis 2023.2 U280 RTL co-simulation with a 3,600-second RTL timeout. It compares every emitted C and RTL output dump against the independent golden and requires a positive measured cycle count. A failed or timed-out job stays in the frozen denominator. No model calls, candidate reselection, or edits to the 216-cell main study occur.

The controller runs **one Vitis worker** in `c2hls_reviewer_ab_rtl_r1_20261001` and uses separate `/tmp/reviewer_ab_rtl_r1_20261001/` scratch and Vitis user-home directories. It does not admit a new job below 100 GiB free scratch, below 128 GiB available memory, above 70% CPU load, when an unrelated Vitis process is present, or when the main and replay sessions already occupy six jobs. The frozen timeout replay treats third-session Vitis as external, so this controller also yields whenever the replay has a pending historical job and fewer than two active historical workers. A current RTL job is allowed to finish; replay may have to wait for that one job before refilling. A 35-second gap between successive jobs gives the replay controller at least one full 20-second poll to admit replacement work. The first worker was started before this priority rule was added and completed with an independent RTL pass. The second worker was then signaled to drain; a separate `c2hls_reviewer_ab_rtl_r1_resume_20261001` tmux session automatically waits for the clean stopped state and resumes the pending queue under the priority and 35-second handoff rules. The original replay and main controllers were not changed.

Control files: `results_sweeps/reviewer_ab_rtl_r1_20261001/{manifest,state,summary}.json` and `launcher.log`. Full per-validation measurement JSON, log, and Vitis archive are in `/mnt/data2/luo00466/c2hls_rl/reviewer_ab_rtl_r1_20261001/raw/`. The `manifest.json` rows map each arm to a `validation_id`, exact source/result hashes, registered harness hashes, and original C-synthesis cycles. Measure RTL cycles only from a completed `cosim.status == pass` record and keep them separate from C-synthesis estimates. Use paired conclusions only if both arms have valid measurements; preserve all missing/failing arms when reporting coverage.

Status: `python3 scripts/run_reviewer_ab_rtl.py status`. Preflight: `python3 scripts/run_reviewer_ab_rtl.py verify` and `python3 scripts/run_reviewer_ab_rtl.py gate`. The controller is a one-worker bounded queue; it can resume from a clean `stopped` state but does not rerun completed jobs automatically.
