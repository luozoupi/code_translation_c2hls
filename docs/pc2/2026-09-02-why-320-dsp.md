# Why 320 DSP on AutoSA mm

One page. Csynth on U280, 3.33 ns. Cosim and P&R later.

## 320 is iso-compute, not device fill

U280 has thousands of DSPs. Rank-1 **320** is **16 PE × SIMD 4 × ~5 DSP per float MAC** at II=1. AutoSA chose that array for 64³ `autosa_mm`, not to fill the chip.

The locked slide holds that iso-compute point: agent stream **4292 / 320** (with-skills) or **4216 / 320** (no-skills JSON) vs AutoSA **4228 / 320**.

## Wider array, worse latency

The 32×8 overlay (`20260830_mm32x8`) already exists. Do not relaunch.

| Array | Stage | Latency | DSP |
|-------|-------|---------|-----|
| 16×4 (locked) | stream | **4292** | **320** |
| 32×8 | compute rewrite | 9782 | 1344 |
| 32×8 | stream | **4583** | **1280** |
| AutoSA rank-1 | — | **4228** | **320** |

More DSP, **worse** finish time than 16×4 stream. The inner PE loop is `pe_kj=512` × two tiles (I/PE=2), so extra workers do not hide the extra I/O.

## Family C is a different search

AutoSA cand 9 (exhaustive HLS, not this agent) is **1846–2161 / 1296 DSP**. Family C’s iso-DSP 32-PE point is **2245 / 1280** — it misses ≤1846. The selected `io4_16x8` is **1571 / 5120**, faster only by oversubscribing DSP. Neither is a 16×4 slide replacement. Do not mix them into the 4228 / 320 figure.
